Privacy

Last updated October 6, 2026

Overview

This policy covers the Getaway Guide app for iPhone and iPad. Getaway Guide keeps everything on your device. It has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics, and nobody, including the developer, receives anything you enter. The rest of this page says exactly what the app stores, where that data can go when you choose to move it, and what the app never does. It took effect on October 6, 2026.

What the app stores on your device

The name or nickname you type, the progress you tick off, your tasks, notes and wish list, your settings, and any photo you attach to a record. The app asks for access to your photo library only when you add a picture, and keeps only the picture you pick. All of it lives in the app's own storage on your device. It is never read by the developer and never leaves the device unless you move it yourself, as described below.

iCloud backup

Under Your data in Setup you can back up to iCloud and restore from it. A backup copies the app's save file into your own iCloud account, under your Apple ID, where only your devices can read it. The developer has no access to it. Nothing is written to iCloud unless you tap Back up now or turn on Back up to iCloud every day, and Restore from iCloud only reads back what you put there. Apple's own privacy policy covers how iCloud itself handles data.

Exports

Export your progress turns your data into text and hands it to the iPhone share sheet, so you can save it to Files or send it wherever you choose. From that point it is in your hands and the app has no further part in it. Load an export reads such a text back in, replacing what is in the app, and keeps a copy of the previous data in case you change your mind.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reset reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No notification server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn it off in Setup or in the phone's Settings at any time.

App updates

When the app opens it checks Expo's update service for a newer version of its own code and downloads it if there is one, the way the App Store keeps apps current. That request carries the app's version and your device's platform, nothing about you or your progress. Beyond that, and Apple's own iCloud and notification features when you turn them on, the app connects to no online service.

Links out

Some records link to the Hello Kitty Island Adventure Wiki and other websites for maps, photos and more detail. Those links open in your browser and the sites you visit are governed by their own privacy policies. The app passes nothing about you to them.

What the app never does

It does not collect personal data from anyone, of any age, including children. It does not track you, use advertising or analytics identifiers, run ads, or sell or share anything with third parties. There is no sign-in and no way for the developer to see who uses the app.

Your control

Everything the app holds is yours to see, change and remove. Erase everything on this phone under Your data in Setup deletes all progress and settings on the device; so does deleting the app. Backups and exports you made stay where you put them until you delete them there. Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request a copy of or to ask the developer to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date in the Overview changes with it and the new text appears here and wherever this policy is published. Changes will never allow data collection without the policy saying so plainly.
